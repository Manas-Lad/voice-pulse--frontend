01 / THE IDEA✳

What is Non-Verbal
Distress Detection?

↗

Non-Verbal Distress Detection is a system designed to identify possible distress during a phone conversation without requiring the person to manually press a panic button.

It analyzes voice stress patterns, breathing changes, and predefined code-word phrases spoken naturally during a conversation.
